Your website content checklist

What we still need from you, and a few things we would like you to confirm before your site goes live.

1. Counsellors we looked at near you

We looked at how other counsellors in Clifton and Bristol present themselves, so your site covers what people expect to find. Nothing from these sites has been added to yours.

Trish WalkerCounselling Directory profile. Explains clearly that bereavement covers all kinds of loss, including anticipatory loss.
Michael Forrest (Bereavement therapy in Clifton listing)Counselling Directory, bereavement therapy in Clifton. Says up front that clients can see him at his Clifton practice room or on Zoom.
Counselling in Clifton, Bristol (Psychology Today)Psychology Today, Clifton listings. Each profile leads with a photo of the counsellor and their fees, which is how many people compare.

2. What we still need, page by page

Every page

  • Your logo, if you have one. For now the browser tab shows a simple green circle with an A.
  • A photo of you. Clients like to see who they will be talking to, and Google can show it beside your site in search results.

Home and About

  • A portrait photo for the Meet Alex section and the About page. They show a calm room for now.
  • Anything more you would like to share about yourself: why you became a counsellor, or what a first session is like. The About page only uses what you sent us.

Counselling pages

  • Your one testimonial is about anxiety, so it only appears on the anxiety page, home, About and Contact. A short quote from a bereavement client and an individual counselling client (with their permission) would help those two pages.
  • Anything you would like to add about how bereavement or individual counselling works with you.

Fees

  • How clients pay you (bank transfer, card) and when.
  • Your cancellation policy, for example how much notice you need.

Contact

  • Your working days and hours, if you would like them shown.
  • Whether there is parking or step-free access at Clifton Wellbeing Rooms.

3. Please confirm

We made these choices to keep the site moving. Please tell us if any of them are wrong.

  • To Query Online sessions are shown at the same price as in-person sessions (60 pounds, concessions 40 pounds). Is that right?
  • To Query The concession rate is shown for students and people on low incomes, with no session length given. Is a concession session also 50 minutes?
  • To Query The photos are calm, illustrative pictures of rooms, a garden and a windowsill, not your own room. Would you like us to use photos of your practice room instead?
  • To Query The map on the Contact page shows the Clifton area. Please check your address is exactly right.
  • To Query We used "Counselling in Bristol and online" as the short line under your name. Happy with that?
  • To Query Would you like to show your BACP membership number? Some clients like to check it on the BACP register.
  • To Query We have not added Google Analytics. If you would like to see visitor numbers, send us your Analytics code.

4. Pages you might like to add

  • Questions people ask: for example what happens on the free call, how online sessions work, and confidentiality.
  • Online counselling: a short page for people outside Bristol, since you work online across the UK.
  • What to expect: your first session, step by step, in your own words.
