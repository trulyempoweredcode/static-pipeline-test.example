Bereavement and loss counselling in Bristol and online

Grief is not something to fix or rush.

A calm space to talk about your loss

Whether the loss was recent or years ago, I offer a calm space to talk about:

  • The person you have lost
  • The relationship
  • The feelings that come with it

Loss can also mean the end of a relationship, a job or your health.

What working together looks like

1

A free 15 minute phone call

The first step is a free 15 minute phone call so we can see whether we are a good fit.

2

Weekly 50 minute sessions

Sessions last 50 minutes, weekly, at the same time each week.

3

Reviewed together every six weeks

There is no fixed number of sessions; we review together every six weeks.
