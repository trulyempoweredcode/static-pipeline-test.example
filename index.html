Meet Alex

An integrative, person-centred way of working

I trained at the University of Bristol (PG Dip Counselling) and work in an integrative, person-centred way. Before counselling I worked as a primary school teacher for ten years.

Sessions last 50 minutes, weekly, at the same time each week. There is no fixed number of sessions; we review together every six weeks.
